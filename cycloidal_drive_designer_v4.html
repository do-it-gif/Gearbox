Loads, stresses and geometry curves

Load model: rigid disc, all pins ideally in contact, load ∝ moment arm [R3]; disc torque = Tout × load share. Contact stress: Hertz line contact [R4]. Stress–strain: Ramberg–Osgood [R7] from typical datasheet values; verify for your exact grade/print settings.

Optimizer

Searches every free variable for the best design by your chosen goal: Zp (from the target ratio or the motor match), ring pin Ø, K1 0.45–0.85, output hole layout, every ticked bearing, and, if you allow it in the sidebar, disc material, disc thickness and housing OD. Designs meeting the target safety factor are ranked first; if none do, it tells you how close it got and what is limiting. Click Apply to load a row.
